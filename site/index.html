Meet Hunter

A steady presence
when life feels uncertain.

Hunter is a Michigan Licensed Master Social Worker with experience in crisis services, behavioral health, and private practice. He has spent much of his career supporting people through some of the most difficult moments of their lives.

That experience informs a calm, respectful approach to therapy. His focus is on helping people feel heard, understand themselves more clearly, and find practical ways through challenging circumstances.

Hunter is especially interested in working with adults who feel overwhelmed, are navigating change, or want space to better understand what they're experiencing.

Cedar Hedge begins as Hunter's solo psychotherapy practice, with care built around the person in front of him.

Behind the name

Rooted in resilience.
Room for connection.

The cedar represents strength and steadiness through changing seasons. It reflects the capacity people have for resilience and growth, even when life is difficult.

The hedge represents boundaries, privacy, and shelter. A protected space that still leaves room to grow and connect.

Together, they reflect the kind of space Cedar Hedge aims to offer.

A few helpful details

Before you
take the next step.

Where will sessions take place?

The practice will begin entirely online, serving clients throughout Michigan through telehealth. Hunter is based in the Interlochen / Traverse City area. There is no in-person office at this time.

Who will the practice work with?

The practice is being developed with a focus on individual counseling for adults. Exact age eligibility is still being finalized and will be shared before scheduling opens.

Will you accept insurance or self-pay?

Hunter plans to offer both insurance and self-pay options. Insurance credentialing is in progress, and no participating plans are confirmed yet. Confirmed coverage and self-pay rates will be posted when available.

Can I request an appointment now?

Not yet. Cedar Hedge Counseling is in the planning stage and is not accepting appointment requests. An opening date has not been set. This site will be updated when scheduling is available.

How will scheduling and intake work?

Once the practice is ready, this website will link to the selected patient portal for scheduling and intake. The system is still being chosen, and this website currently has no patient information form.

Is Cedar Hedge a crisis service?

No. Hunter's crisis-work experience informs his approach, but Cedar Hedge Counseling is a psychotherapy practice, not an emergency or immediate-response service.

If you need immediate support in the U.S., call or text 988. For an immediate emergency, call 911.
